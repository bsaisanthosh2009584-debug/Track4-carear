THE CHALLENGE

Why Do Students Struggle to Become Industry-Ready?

Education builds knowledge. Industry demands skills. Track4Career is designed to bridge that gap.

🎓 Skills Are Hard to Measure

Students often don't have a clear picture of their current skills, strengths, and areas that need improvement.

🔗 Academia & Industry Are Disconnected

Industry requirements continuously evolve, while academic learning may not always reflect those changes.

🎯 Opportunities Are Not Personalized

Finding the right internship, project, or career opportunity can be difficult without skill-based guidance.
